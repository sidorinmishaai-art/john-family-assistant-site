John Family Assistant

John is a private, self-hosted family assistant. It helps its owner and invited family members create reminders and manage Google Calendar events from Telegram messages.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Data accessed. With a user’s explicit Google authorization, John can read calendar information and create or update calendar events needed to carry out that user’s request.

Purpose. Calendar data is used only to provide requested assistant features such as checking availability and creating reminders or events. It is not sold and is not used for advertising.

Processing and storage. The assistant runs on private self-hosted infrastructure. OAuth credentials are stored encrypted. Information may be sent to the AI provider configured by the owner only when needed to interpret or fulfill a user request.

Sharing. Data is not disclosed to unrelated third parties. Service providers receive only the information required to operate the requested feature.

Retention and deletion. Connection data is retained while the integration is active. A user can revoke access in Google Account security settings and request deletion through the support contact shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.

Security. Access is limited to authorized family members, secrets are kept outside source control, and calendar operations use Google OAuth.

Contact. Use the support address displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
